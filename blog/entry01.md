# Entry 1: My Freedom Project Tool (Phaser)
##### 10/7/24

-----
The tool I'm going to be using for my year-long freedom project is phaser. I chose [phaser](https://phaser.io) as it was the best for game making and I had many ideas I wanted to create. A few being a pet management simulator, where you run a pet shop, another being a cat trading card game, something similar to pokemon, my last idea was a pet simulator where you care for a pet feeding, accessorizing and playing with them.

Some games I looked at included:  [Ethgard Legends]([https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/health/wellness-and-prevention/keep-your-brain-young-with-music#:~:text=It%20provides%20a%20total%20brain,%2C%20mental%20alertness%2C%20and%20memory.](https://phaser.io/news/2025/04/ethgard-legends)) which was uploaded to phaser on 17 April, 2025. Ethgard Legends is what made me think of the cat trading card game. [Princess Pet Studio](https://phaser.io/news/2017/07/princess-pet-studio) uploaded 5 July, 2017. This is initially what I was thinking of creating because it's a very cute like pet dress up game which I thought was cool is definitely an idea to hold onto and something I can come back to.

## Engineering Design Process

I am currently in the first stage of the Engineering Design Process (EDP), where I need to figure out how to use then practice phaser and start off with a demo game, where I can practice making anything just to see what game I want to go for. 

## Skills

One skill I developed during this freedom project is creativity, because seeing all these amazing games and all the different type of genres is truly inspiring and I aspire to create something similar to what other people have made or unique, but as long as I am proud of what I made I would be happy.

Another skill I improved is collaboration. This is because I was initially stuck on how to even download phaser to begin practicing or testing anything, so there would be no point in researching games if I couldn't create anything.

## Summary

All in all, I am pretty excited for my first game, I wanna make lots of different games but mostly animal games like the pet shop or the cat trading card games I mentioned earlier but I first to need to tinker and understand phaser but as soon as I'm done with that I'm going all out on the games.

## Next Steps
I look forward to continuing to find games on phaser and making my first one, I really hope I don't get lazy and make the bare minimum or worse as this seems like a really fun project but I continue to procrastinate heavily, I'm even doing this blog very late into the night.
